Wisp

Free, open-source firmware that brings the Spotify Car Thing back to life, with its Android companion app, Wisp Companion.

  • Spotify, YouTube Music and SoundCloud on the Car Thing's screen and knob
  • Turn-by-turn directions, calls and notifications from your phone
  • On-device voice commands
  • Updates over the air

Wisp Companion can connect to your YouTube Music library through the YouTube Data API, so the Car Thing can show your playlists and liked music and like songs or add them to a playlist when you ask. Your sign-in stays on your phone. See the privacy policy for exactly what Wisp does with it.
