Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Wisp is free, open-source firmware for the Spotify Car Thing, with an Android companion app, Wisp Companion. This page explains what Wisp does with your information. The short version: Wisp has no servers and collects nothing. Everything stays on your own phone and your own Car Thing.

YouTube Music

If you choose to connect YouTube Music, Wisp Companion asks Google for permission to use your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API. You sign in on Google's own page, in your own browser, at google.com/device.

  • What Wisp reads: your playlists and the songs in them, and the music videos you've liked. It also asks whether you've liked the song that's playing.
  • What Wisp changes, only when you ask it to: it likes or un-likes the song that's playing, and adds the song that's playing to a playlist you name.
  • Where it goes: the sign-in is stored only in Wisp Companion's private storage on your phone. The Car Thing receives song and playlist names and cover pictures over your phone's Bluetooth or USB connection, to show them on its screen. Nothing is sent to the people who make Wisp, or to anyone other than Google.
  • What Wisp never does: it doesn't sell, share or transfer your YouTube data, use it for advertising, or keep it anywhere but your phone and Car Thing.
  • Removing access: tap Sign out in Wisp Companion, which deletes the sign-in from your phone and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove Wisp at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling Wisp Companion deletes everything it stored.

Wisp's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By connecting YouTube Music you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Everything else

  • Spotify, SoundCloud and other services are signed in to on your phone or Car Thing, and talk directly to those services.
  • Navigation, notifications, calls and your phone's battery are passed from your phone to your Car Thing only, to show on its screen.
  • Voice commands are recognised on the Car Thing itself, not in the cloud.
  • Updates are downloaded from GitHub, which sees the request like any download.
  • Wisp has no analytics, ads or tracking.

Children

Wisp isn't directed at children under 13.
